# VAT, parts 2a and 2b (Doc 10 E14 R3 part 2; Doc 09 PR-32)

**Where it comes from in the specification:**
- **Doc 09 PR-32:** a taxed sale debits receivables or cash with the gross amount, and credits revenue with the net and output VAT payable with the VAT. Accounts 1310 (input VAT recoverable) and 2200 (output VAT payable) are reserved for R3.
- **Doc 12 SCR-24:** tax settings are rebuilt in R3.
- **Doc 16:** tax rules carry the date they apply from, so past documents stay reproducible.

**Owner decisions of 17 November 2026:**
- VAT is off unless the business says it is VAT-registered.
- If it is: 7.5% standard, and each item is standard, zero-rated or exempt.
- Till prices include VAT. Each invoice says whether its prices include VAT.
- WHT deducted by customers is recorded too (part 2c).

## The three steps

| Step | What | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2a** | Settings › Taxes, item VAT classes, the VAT accounts, output VAT on till sales and invoices (including bookings, jobs and credit sales, which invoice through the same code), and the VAT return | Done |
| **2b** | Input VAT on supplier bills, bills for received goods and expenses | Done |
| **2c** | VAT taken back by refunds and credit notes; VAT on offline sales; WHT on customer payments (account 1320, flagged as unreviewed) | After 2b |

**Until 2c:** a refund of a sale, a credit note on an invoice, or a reversal of an expense that carried VAT is refused with "Refunds and credit notes for documents with VAT are not available yet." Posting them without taking the VAT back would leave the VAT return wrong. Documents without VAT refund and credit as before.

## What happens

**Settings › Taxes** (`/settings/taxes`; Owner or Admin with `settings.edit`):
- The business turns on "registered for VAT", and gives its VAT number and the date it started charging.
- The date can't be before the books started, or in a closed month.
- Turning VAT on adds three accounts to the chart: 1310 Input VAT recoverable, 1320 Withholding tax credits and 2200 Output VAT payable.
- Each item can then be marked standard rate, zero-rated or exempt. Items not marked are standard rate.
- Documents already recorded are not changed.

**At the till:** the customer pays the shelf price. CACU works out the VAT inside it, at rate ÷ (100 + rate) of the standard-rated part, rounded to the kobo. Cash, transfer, split and credit sales all work this way.

**Invoices:**
- Each invoice says whether its prices include VAT. Without a choice, it follows the business's setting.
- The invoice stores the net (`subtotal`), the VAT (`tax`) and the gross (`total`).
- Payments, ageing and statements use the gross, as before.

**Reports › VAT return** (`/reports/vat-return`):
- For one month, it shows sales and purchases split into standard-rated (before VAT), zero-rated and exempt, the VAT on each, and what is payable.
- It checks that the VAT accounts in the ledger moved by exactly those amounts, and says so if they didn't.
- It is prepared for the owner to check. CACU does not file VAT returns.

## Input VAT (2b)

The VAT a business can reclaim is what the supplier's document shows, so it is entered rather than worked out:

- **Bills and bills for received goods:** the amounts are before VAT. "VAT on the bill" is added, so the supplier is owed both.
- **Expenses:** the amount is what was paid in total. "VAT inside the amount" is part of it, as on a till receipt.
- **The VAT field** appears only while the business charges VAT. A button offers the standard-rate amount (7.5% on the amount, or 7.5/107.5 of a total).
- **The API refuses:**
  - VAT entered while the business isn't registered on that date. The VAT is then part of the cost.
  - VAT above what the standard rate allows on the amount.
  - VAT on an expense recorded offline: such an expense is recorded online.
- **Posting:** the VAT is debited to 1310 Input VAT recoverable, and the rest to the expense or Goods received not invoiced. A taxed bill or expense posts as one entry crediting payables or the bank with the total, and payments are matched to it as to any bill.
- **Locks on account 1310:** only a bill or an expense can debit it, with one VAT line. Reclaims or settlements that credit it stay possible.
- **On the return:** purchases with VAT count as standard-rated. Purchases with no VAT, while registered, count under "exempt" (no VAT charged). VAT payable is output VAT less input VAT; a negative figure is reclaimable.
- **For forms:** `GET /vat/status` tells any member's screens whether VAT applies today, and at what rate.

## How it is posted

- **Rounding:** VAT is worked out per document and rounded half up to the kobo. The ledger, the document and the return therefore agree exactly. The accounting library's own tax feature would post unrounded amounts, such as ₦69.7674, so CACU doesn't use it.
- **One entry per taxed document:** a sale or invoice with VAT posts as one balanced journal entry. It debits the till, bank, clearing or receivable account with the gross, and credits revenue with the net and 2200 with the VAT. Receipts are matched to it as to any invoice. Documents without VAT post exactly as before.
- **Locks on account 2200:**
  - Only a sale or an invoice can credit 2200, with exactly one VAT line.
  - Paying VAT to the tax office (a debit to 2200) stays possible.
  - An invoice is posted only if its stored VAT equals its VAT line.
- **The return's record:** `accounting.document_vat` gets one row per taxed document, in the same transaction as its posting. It holds the amounts by class, the VAT and the rate. The return adds these up.
- **Rates:** `accounting.vat_rate` holds each rate with the date it applies from (7.5% standard from 1 February 2020). A document uses the rate in force on its date.

## Tests

- `backend/tests/test_vat_postgres.py`:
  - Nothing changes until the business registers; who can register, and which dates are allowed.
  - Cash, transfer and zero-rated sales: the VAT, the ledger and the shelf price.
  - Invoices with and without VAT included, paid in full; credit sales.
  - The VAT return agrees with the trial balance.
  - Refunds and credit notes of taxed documents are refused.
  - Output VAT can't be posted by other documents.
  - Input VAT (2b): needs registration and stays within the rate; expenses and bills claim it, and the supplier is owed it; bills are paid in full; bills for received goods claim it too.
  - The return nets input VAT against output VAT and agrees with the trial balance.
  - Reversing a taxed expense waits for 2c; input VAT can't be claimed by journals.
- `src/tests/security/vat-ui.test.tsx`: turning VAT on, marking items, and the return, including a disagreement with the books.
- `src/tests/security/vat-field.test.tsx`: the VAT field on purchase forms, and the most VAT allowed.
