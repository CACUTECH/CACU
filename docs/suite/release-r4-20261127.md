# R4 release notes: payroll to the books, fixed assets, tax and year end

Merged to `main` in PRs #98 and #99 (branch `feat/r3-books`, up to commit `ec457da`, 27 November 2026). None of it is in production yet: production needs the migrations below, applied with approval.

## What is in it

**Payroll to the books (FR-203)**
- An approved HR payroll run is posted to the books in a separate **Post** step. Pay and remittances are recorded from the till or a bank.
- Each run has remittance schedules for PAYE, pension, NHF and NHIS.
- Employees open their payslips through private links that expire and can be revoked.
- An Owner can reverse a payroll payment entered in error.
- Behind `accounting.payroll_switch`, which stays off until a professional signs off.

**Fixed assets (IAS 16)**
- Assets are bought paid, bought on credit, or brought in as owned before CACU, with input VAT claimed where the business is registered.
- Depreciation is straight-line or reducing balance, through a monthly **Post depreciation** step. Month close blocks until it is posted.
- Disposals record proceeds and a gain or loss, and charge output VAT when the business is VAT-registered. On the cash flow, proceeds show under investing.
- An Owner can remove an asset entered in error.

**Capital allowances (Nigeria Tax Act 2025)**
- Tax depreciation runs on the asset register at 10%, 20% or 25% by class, keeping 1% until disposal, with balancing charges and allowances.
- Part business use claims only the business share.
- An asset also used to earn income that is not taxable claims only the taxable share once that income is 10% or more of the year's income.
- Nothing here posts to the books.
- Behind `accounting.capital_allowance_switch`, which stays off.

**Company income tax**
- A yearly estimate from the books, showing every step:
  - the small-company test (₦50m turnover);
  - companies income tax at 30% and the development levy at 4%;
  - losses brought forward;
  - withholding tax credits;
  - the business's own year-end month.
- Once a year has ended, the Owner posts its tax, records payments, and can reverse mistakes.
- Behind `accounting.income_tax_switch`, which stays off.

**Year-end close**
- The Owner closes a finished year once all its months are closed, and years close in order.
- Closing locks the year's months.
- No closing journal is posted. The balance sheet shows closed years' profit as "Retained earnings from closed years" and the rest as "Profit for the year".
- The Owner can reopen the latest closed year, with a reason.

**Preview business**
- CACU picks one business that sees every module and every switched-off feature, so the team can show and try them before release.

**Fixes**
- Profitability counts stock cost and completed jobs from midnight in the business's timezone, not UTC.
- Posting checks give each entry's own message when the business has no till, or when the account given is not a bank.
- The income tax estimate leaves out the posted tax expense when working out profit before tax.

   ```sql
   SELECT id, name, created_at FROM public.businesses WHERE name ILIKE '%<preview business name>%';
   INSERT INTO accounting.preview_business(business_id, reason) VALUES ('<id>', 'CACU preview and demo business');
   ```
2. **Check the switches are off:** `payroll_switch`, `capital_allowance_switch`, `income_tax_switch` and `funding_switch` should all show `enabled = false`.
3. **Smoke test in the preview business:**
   - Post a depreciation month.
   - Open the capital allowances card and the income tax estimate.
   - Close an old month and look at the Years section on the periods page.

## Before the switches go on

**Tax professional:** capital allowances and income tax follow CACU's reading of the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. The open questions are listed in `docs/suite/capital-allowances-20261207.md` and the income tax doc. In short, a tax professional needs to confirm:
- **Rates and classes:** agricultural equipment and intangibles, where the sources disagree.
- **Allowance years:** a full year's allowance in the year an asset is bought, and none in the year it is sold.
- **Balancing allowances:** whether they are still given.
- **The 1% kept:** that it stays until disposal.
- **Assets owned before 2026:** the transitional rule.
- **Private use:** that only the business share is claimed and the rest is lost.
- **Non-taxable income:** the measure (income, not use of the asset) and the 10% test.
- **Small companies:** the ₦50m turnover threshold (the June 2026 guidelines say ₦100m), and whether the fixed assets test uses carrying amount or cost.

Once confirmed, set `reviewed_at` on `accounting.capital_allowance_rate` and `accounting.income_tax_rule`, then turn the switches on.

**Payroll professional:** posting, remittances and payslips before `payroll_switch` goes on.
