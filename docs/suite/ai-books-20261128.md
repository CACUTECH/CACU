# AI on the books, part 1: read-only tools (E18 R3)

Doc 15 has the assistant answer owners' questions about their own business. The model calls read-only tools and explains what they return. It never computes a figure, sees another business, or posts anything (PRIN-07, ADR-009). This part builds the tools. Part 2 is the gateway that calls the model, part 3 the evaluation sets, and part 4 the screen.

**Gate G4.** Nothing here calls a model. Business answers stay off for customers until two things are true: data processing terms are signed with the AI provider, and the Doc 15 evaluation bars are met.

## Routes

- `GET /api/v1/businesses/{id}/ai/tools` lists the tools this member may use, each with its inputs as JSON Schema. The gateway offers the model only these.
- `GET /api/v1/businesses/{id}/ai/tools/{tool}?…` runs one tool.
  - The tool keeps the permission of the screen its figures come from, checked in the same database transaction. So the assistant can never show a member more than the screens do.
  - Inputs are strict: unknown fields, values over 40 characters and more than 6 fields are refused.

## Tools

| Tool | Inputs | Permission | Built on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `profit_summary` | period | ledger.view | the statements, with the period before |
| `cash_position` | as-of date | ledger.view | cash and bank ledger, pending transfers, the last 30 days in and out |
| `top_products` | period, metric, limit ≤ 20 | sales.view | profitability; cost and margin only with ledger.view |
| `slow_stock` | days | inventory.view | stock valuation and the last sale of each item |
| `top_expenses` | period, limit ≤ 20 | ledger.view | the statements |
| `receivables` | as-of date | invoices.view | the ageing report |
| `lapsed_customers` | days | customers.view | the customer facts behind segments |
| `budget_variance` | month | ledger.view | budget against actual |
| `cash_forecast` | weeks ≤ 8 | ledger.view | cash now and recent weekly averages, method stated |
| `unusual_spending` | month | ledger.view | each expense against its three months before |

## What every result carries

- **Period and source (AI-002):** the period, or the date the position is at, and the screen and path the figures come from.
- **Limitations (AI-003):** what the figures cannot see, which the answer must state:
  - books not started, or started after the period began;
  - transfers not yet confirmed;
  - offline sales waiting for review;
  - bank lines in the period not yet matched;
  - statement exceptions.
- **Personal data (AI-005):** customer names only. No phone numbers, emails or bank details.
- **Names are inert (AI-007):** names from the books are stripped of markup, control and format characters (such as right-to-left overrides), and cut to 60 characters.
- **The label:** "From your CACU books, not reviewed".

## Choices made

- **Cash forecast:** the forecast is cash now plus the average weekly money in and out over the last 84 days, or fewer if the books are younger. What customers owe and what is owed to suppliers by then are shown separately and not added in, because the averages already include past collections and payments.
- **Unusual spending:** an expense is unusual when it is more than two standard deviations above its three-month average. When the earlier months were flat, it must also be more than 20% above that average. Cost of goods sold is left out, because it follows sales. Months before the books began are unknown, not zero, so nothing is compared until three full months are booked.

## Fixed on the way

`budgets.against_actual` reported `has_budget: true` even when no budget had been saved. It read a defaultdict before testing whether it was empty, which creates entries. As a result, the budget-against-actual screen never showed its "no budget yet" notice. A regression check is in `test_budgets_postgres.py`.
