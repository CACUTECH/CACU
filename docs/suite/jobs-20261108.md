# Bookings, part 4: jobs (Doc 10)

Jobs are now saved to the business; before, the screen kept them only in the browser. A job collects the services and materials used, and the time each person spent. Completing it takes the materials out of stock. A completed job is then paid at the till or billed by invoice. On Chairman, the job shows its profit.

## Jobs (`/jobs` and `/jobs/{id}`)

- **New job:**
  - a title;
  - a customer, or a walk-in name;
  - optionally a staff member, a due date and notes.
- **Numbers** are given by the database: JOB-0001, JOB-0002 and so on.
- **Lines:** any active, priced catalogue item. Services are services; products are materials. Each item appears once, with a quantity.
- **Labour:** time (hours and minutes) per staff member, for profit.
- **States:**
  - Open → In progress → Completed.
  - Open or In progress → Cancelled, which needs a reason.
  - Lines and labour change only while a job is open or in progress.
- **Completing** happens through the accounting API, with the books on:
  - each tracked material leaves stock at its cost (debit cost of sales, credit inventory, through the same valuation rules as a sale);
  - the total is recorded as the job's materials cost;
  - a job with too little stock is refused, saying how many are on hand;
  - a job with no lines can't be completed.
- **Billed once:**
  - **Take payment** opens the till (`/pos?job=…`) with the job's lines. The sale must be exactly those lines, and discounts within the person's limit still apply.
  - **Bill by invoice** issues an invoice at catalogue prices, numbered like the job (JOB-0001), to the job's customer. A walk-in job asks for a customer.
  - Either way, materials aren't issued from stock again.
- **Doc 10 differences:**
  - Doc 10 lists "Invoiced" as a state. A billed job keeps its Completed state and shows "Paid at the till" or "Invoiced", as bookings do.
  - The invoice is issued straight away rather than left as a draft, so the job can be billed once and its materials can't be issued twice.

## Job profit (Chairman)

- **Profit** = charge at catalogue prices − materials at the cost they left stock at (estimated at current cost before completion) − labour at each staff member's cost per hour.
- **Cost rates:** the owner sets each staff member's cost per hour on Staff and hours. Only the owner sees them. They aren't linked to payroll until R4.
- **Who sees profit:** people who can see the books (`ledger.view`), on the Chairman plan (`module.job_costing`). Others see the charge and materials cost only.
- **Missing rates:** labour by someone without a rate counts as zero, and the job says so.

## Who can do what

| Action | Permission |
|---|---|
| See jobs | `services.view` |
| Open a job, add lines and time | `services.create` |
| Start, change, remove lines, cancel, complete | `services.edit` |
| Bill by invoice | `invoices.post` |
| Take payment at the till | `sales.post` |
| Set staff cost rates | Owner, on Chairman |

## Database

Migration `20261108120000_jobs.sql`:
- **`public.jobs`** gains these columns: `number`, `customer_name`, `staff_id`, `created_by`, `started_at`, `completed_at`, `cancel_reason`, `materials_cost`, `bill_kind`, `bill_id`, `billed_at`. Old policies are replaced and the delete grant is removed.
- **New tables:** `public.job_lines` and `public.job_labour`, with row security.
- **Rule triggers:**
  - `jobs_rules`: numbering, states and the reason to cancel.
  - Completion and billing only through the accounting API.
  - `job_parts_rules`: lines and labour only while open; active catalogue items and staff.
- **`accounting.staff_cost_rate`.**
- **`accounting.job_costing_allowed()`:** SECURITY DEFINER for the runtime, like the shifts plan check.
- **`transactions.job_id`.**

The test schema creates the legacy `jobs` table with production's checks.

## Code changes elsewhere

- Billing a job must not issue its materials again. So `post_command`, `invoice_create` and `sale_create` take a set of "already issued" items, which the stock check and the stock issue leave out.
- `POST /sales` accepts `job_id`.

## API

- `GET /jobs/{id}`: lines, the charge, materials cost and, where allowed, profit.
- `POST /jobs/{id}/complete`.
- `POST /jobs/{id}/invoice`.
- `GET /jobs/cost-rates` and `POST /jobs/cost-rates`.

## Tests

- `test_jobs_postgres.py` (3 cases, on a business started from the real chart):
  - **Completion:** numbering; a cashier opens a job but can't run it; completion refused through the Data API; materials leave stock at cost through the API, once; lines locked after; too little stock refused; cancelling needs a reason; an empty job can't be completed.
  - **Billing:** at the till the sale must match the lines and stock isn't issued again; billed once; an incomplete job can't be invoiced; an invoice for the job; billing can't be undone through the Data API.
  - **Profit:** unrated labour, owner-only cost rates, profit after completion, hidden from a cashier, and off below Chairman.
- Jest `jobs-ui`:
  - error wording and the till cart check;

  - paying at the till and billing by invoice;
