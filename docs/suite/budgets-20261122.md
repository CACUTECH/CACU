# Budgets (Doc 10 E14 R3 part 3; Doc 03 FR-137, FR-145)

**Where it comes from in the specification:**
- **Doc 03 FR-137:** budgets.
- **Doc 03 FR-145:** budget against actual.
- **Doc 11 R3:** CRUD `/budgets`, and `GET /reports/budget-vs-actual`.
- **Doc 12 SCR-18:** the budgets and profitability screens, rebuilt in R3. Until now they were "not available yet" pages.

No owner decision was needed. The design follows the usual setup for small businesses.

## What happens

**Analytics › Planning & Budgeting** (`/analytics/planning`):
- One budget per financial year. The year starts in the month set for the books.
- Each income and expense account in the chart has an amount for each of the year's twelve months.
- The owner can type a yearly amount, which is spread evenly to the kobo; the last month takes the remainder, so the months add back up exactly. Or they can open "By month" and set each month.
- The screen shows the year's planned income, spending and profit.
- Saving replaces the whole budget for that year, and is recorded in the audit log.
- Only the Owner or an Admin can save. The route also needs `ledger.edit`. Anyone with `ledger.view` can read the budget.
- A suspended (read-only) workspace can still save its budget, since it isn't a trading document.

**Analytics › Budget vs. Actual** (`/analytics/budget-vs-actual`):
- For a chosen month, and for the financial year up to the end of that month, each account shows its budget, the actual figure from the ledger, and the difference with a percentage.
- Income is shown as earned and spending as spent.
- **On track** (green) means income at or above budget, or spending at or below it. Off track is shown in red.
- Totals for income and spending, and a profit line.
- Accounts with no budget and no activity are left out. Accounts with activity but no budget, such as cost of sales, are shown, so the profit line matches the books.
- Without a budget for the year, the page says so and links to Planning.

## How it is stored

- **Tables:** `accounting.budget` holds one row per business and year, with who saved it and when. `accounting.budget_line` holds the account, month and amount; only amounts above zero are stored. Both have row-level security like the other accounting tables.
- **Account check:** the accounting API checks that each budget line is an income or expense account of the business's chart.
- **Actual figures:** the ledger's movement on each account between the period's dates, summed by account.
