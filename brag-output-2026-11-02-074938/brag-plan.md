# CACU brag video plan

## Answers

- **What it is:** CACU is a business operating system for Nigerian small businesses. You sell, and every sale becomes real double-entry books automatically.
- **Who it's for:** owner-run shops, salons and traders who sell every day but keep their records in a notebook.
- **What it does for them:** profit they can trust, and statements a lender can read, with no accounting knowledge.
- **What sets it apart:** competitors are either a POS app or an accountant's tool. CACU puts a real ledger under an easy till.
- **Strongest claim:** you ring up two tins of milk, and the books post themselves and balance to ₦0.00.
- **Visual hook:** a navy screen asking "You sell all day. Who keeps the books?", answered by the CACU logo assembling.
- **Real UI to show:**
  - the POS screen ("POS Checkout", "Search inventory or scan SKU...", "Confirm & Record Sale", "Sale Recorded");
  - the accountant view of the posting, using real chart codes 1000, 4000, 5000 and 1200;
  - the trial balance difference;
  - statements labelled "Management statements", exported to PDF and Excel.
- **Tone:** default. Punchy, clean, confident.
- **Share line:** "Sell. CACU keeps the books."

## Angle

The notebook problem, answered by a till that does the bookkeeping. The hook asks the question every shop owner dodges. The product answers it in three beats: entry (a sale on the phone), key action (it posts itself as balanced books) and result (statements a lender can read).

## Identity

- **Colours:**
  - CACU navy `#09006C` and purple `#5C40FF`, sampled from the supplied logo; the purple is also the app's `--primary`, DEC-005.
  - Light `#F7F7F7`, matching the logo file's background.
  - Soft lilac `#A99BFF` for accents.
- **Fonts:** Space Grotesk for headings and Inter for UI, both the app's own bundled fonts.
- **Logo:** the supplied wordmark and mark images. The mark's two shapes are animated by clipping the real image.
- **Stand-ins:** all shop data is fictional: "Ada Provisions", generic items (milk tin ₦650, noodles carton ₦9,500, garri 1 kg ₦1,800) and illustrative costs. No real customer data.

## Music

- **Tempo:** 120 BPM, one beat = 0.5 s. 42 beats = 21.0 s.
- **Chords:** C – Am – F – G, one bar (2 s) each.
- **Arrangement:** the hook is sparse (pad and muted kick), and the full groove drops on the reveal at 3.0 s.
- **Sound effects** are in key and on the grid:
  - whooshes into each cut;
  - soft plucks on the taps;
  - a C–E–G chime on "Sale Recorded";
  - marimba ticks on each ledger line;
  - a low stamp on ₦0.00;
  - pops on PDF and Excel;
  - a final ring-out.
