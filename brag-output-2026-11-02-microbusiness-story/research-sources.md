# Research and claim register
Accessed 2 November 2026. No interviews conducted; no real business or person is portrayed as a CACU customer.

1. Olajide, M. & Obialo, F.-K. (2020), Effects of Record Keeping on the Growth of Small Scale Enterprises in Abeokuta South Local Government Area, Ogun State, Nigeria. JEMT 26(5), 34?43. https://journaljemt.com/index.php/JEMT/article/view/868 ; DOI https://doi.org/10.9734/jemt/2020/v26i530254 . The study purposively selected 120 of 170 registered businesses and reports 59.2% maintained financial records manually themselves without professional help. Historical local sample; not representative of all Nigeria, nor a current prevalence estimate. The film uses that one result, with its year and limitations on screen.

2. Abiola Oyebanjo / International Growth Centre (policy brief published 5 March 2025), Enhancing initial adoption of digital records and best practices among small firms: Evidence from Nigeria. https://www.theigc.org/publications/enhancing-initial-adoption-digital-records-and-best-practices-among-small-firms . The brief reports greater consistency (precision) in profit recall following digital record adoption and highlights personalised support. The film paraphrases this cautiously and does not use the 95% adoption statistic, which concerns the pilot comparison rather than a general improvement in profit. No CACU-specific causal effectiveness is inferred.

## Story boundaries
Bisi is an explicitly labelled fictional composite used to explain a plausible manual-record workflow. No quote, image, interview, endorsement or testimonial from a real operator is invented. Scattered records, forgotten entries and evening arithmetic are storytelling inferences, not additional measured research results. All transaction amounts are fictional. Sales120,000 ? cost80,000 ? expenses20,000 =20,000 is a simplified arithmetic example before other charges. No tax, loan eligibility, profit increase, time saving or compliance guarantee is asserted.

3. Primary working paper behind the IGC brief: Oyebanjo, January 2024, Beyond Beneficial Information: The Impact of Additional Consulting on Digital Record Adoption and Profit Measurement. https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-07/Oyebanjo%20Working%20paper%20January%202024.pdf .
