# CACU brag video plan: "Mama Ada's Exercise Book"

## Answers

- **What it is:** CACU is a business operating system for Nigerian micro businesses. You sell on your phone, and your books keep themselves.
- **Who it's for:** owners like Ada, a composite Ikorodu provisions-shop owner whose sales and credit live in a blue-biro exercise book.
- **What it does for them:** prices from her list, credit customers she can actually track, books that start from plain questions, and statements she can take to the bank.
- **What sets it apart:** a real ledger under an easy till, not just a POS app.
- **Strongest moment:** "will pay Friday", then "Friday was three Fridays ago". Later the payoff, "Still owes ₦3,000", now that it's tracked.
- **Visual hook:** a real-looking exercise book page with the credit list at the back.
- **Real UI to show:**
  - the POS with its real wording ("POS Checkout", "Confirm & Record Sale", "Sale Recorded");
  - an invoice with a part payment;
  - the real Start your books question "How much cash is in the till and safe now?";
  - the "Management statements" export to PDF.
- **Tone:** default, warm and human. A short story film, not a feature list.
- **Share line:** "Will pay Friday." Friday was three Fridays ago. CACU: Grow With Ease.

## Angle

A story in three acts: the exercise book, CACU, and the punchline that the book is now for homework.

The research (Moniepoint 2025 Informal Economy Report, the World Bank Enterprise Survey via BusinessDay, and NTAA s.31) shaped the story. None of its statistics appear on screen.

The roadmap features (offline selling, WhatsApp reminders, funding readiness) are left out to keep this film around 25 s. They're in the share-copy variants.

## Identity

- **Colours:** navy `#09006C` and purple `#5C40FF` (from the logo and the app), light `#F7F7F7`, lilac `#A99BFF`. The exercise book is cream `#FFF8E1` with blue rules and a red margin.
- **Fonts:** Space Grotesk (headings) and Inter (UI), the app's bundled fonts. The handwriting in the book is Inter italic in "biro blue" `#1F3FA8`.
- **Tagline:** "Grow With Ease" is the main tagline; "Sell. CACU keeps the books." is the supporting line.
- **Fictional data:** Ada, Mama T, Brother Sule, all goods and all amounts are invented.

## Music

- **Tempo:** 120 BPM; one beat is 0.5 s; 50 beats make 25.0 s.
- **Chords:** C – Am – F – G.
- **Arrangement:** the hook is a soft pad with a muted pulse, and the groove drops on the reveal at 6.5 s.
- **Effects:** all in key and on the grid:
  - whooshes into each cut;
  - marimba ticks on the three numbers;
  - plucks on the taps;
  - a C–E–G chime on "Sale Recorded";
  - a pop on "₦3,000" and on PDF;
  - a final ring-out under the logo.
