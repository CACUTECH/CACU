# Brag Plan: CACU

## What is this app?

CACU is a business operating system for Nigerian small businesses. You sell, manage stock, invoice and run your team in one place, and every transaction becomes real double-entry books automatically.

## The angle

A guided tour, from the notebook to books an owner can trust:
- the problem: records scattered across a notebook, a bank alert and a WhatsApp chat;
- what's live today, in the order an owner meets it: set up, stock, sell, get paid, spend, start your books, real books, statements, team and HR, trust;
- what's coming next.

The narrator is a calm, warm guide. The screen always shows the real UI wording from the app (POS Checkout, Confirm & Record Sale, Sale Recorded, the Start your books questions, Trial balance difference, Management statements).

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)

A navy screen: "You sell all day. / Who keeps the books?" Then the scraps of a real trading day land one by one: a notebook page, a bank alert and a WhatsApp message.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **Setup:** the business name is typed in and the owner picks "Products".
- **Stock:** an adjustment waits for approval, then is approved by the owner.
- **Selling:** a POS sale on a phone. Two taps, Cash, Confirm & Record Sale, then "Sale Recorded".
- **Credit sales:** an ₦100,000 invoice, a ₦40,000 part payment, ₦60,000 still owed.
- **Spending:** a supplier bill, an expense, and a reversal with a reason.
- **Start your books:** the real questions, then Approve.
- **Real books:** the posting in the accountant view, "Trial balance difference ₦0.00", and the "reviewed" badge on the standard chart.
- **Statements:** the statement tabs, comparatives, PDF and Excel, and weighted-average cost.
- **Team and HR:** an invitation with roles, and a payroll run on the 2026 tax bands, approved.
- **Trust:** data kept separate, records that can't be changed, uploads checked.
- **Roadmap** (navy "Coming next" world):
  - transfers that wait for confirmation, offline selling, the three plans through Paystack;
  - purchasing, bookings with deposits and WhatsApp reminders, cashier shifts, roles for cashiers and accountants, bank reconciliation, budgets, VAT;
  - AI answers from your books, funding readiness shared only with consent, then payroll posting, e-invoicing and new countries.

## Outro / punchline

Back to the light ground. The logo, "Books an owner can trust." and "Sell. CACU keeps the books." The music rings out.

## User flow worth showing

Set up → ring up a sale on the phone → the sale becomes balanced books → statements a lender can read.

## Tone

- **Preset:** polished.
- **Creative direction:** a calm product tour for owners, part launch film, part demo.
- **Interpretation:** fewer, longer holds per scene. Narration carries the detail and the screen shows the product working. There are no jokes; confidence comes from specifics.

## Format: landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps

## Duration: about 190 s

The user asked for a 3-minute film covering every live and planned feature. This overrides brag's 15–25 s law; the narration is the clock.

## Visual identity (from the project)

- **Grounds:** light `#F7F7F7` for live features (the logo file's own background); navy `#09006C` for the hook and the "coming next" world.
- **Accent:** `#5C40FF` (the app's `--primary`, DEC-005, and the logo's dome). Lilac `#A99BFF` on navy.
- **Text:** navy `#09006C` on light, white on navy, and muted `#5D5B7A`.
- **Fonts:** Space Grotesk for display and Inter for UI, using the app's bundled variable font files.
- **Strongest visual element:** the logo's two shapes (purple dome, navy bowl), and the phone POS.

## Audio direction

- **Role:** a warm bed under the narration. The narration is the clock.
- **Music:**
  - Part 1, live today (scenes 1–12): "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 1", 120 BPM.
  - Part 2, coming next (scenes 13–17): "Vol. 12", 110 BPM, so the music changes as the story turns to the future.
  - Credit both as: "Music: Sascha Ende, ende.app (CC BY 4.0)".
- **Music treatment:**
  - 0.45 during the opening title, ducked to 0.14 under the voice.
  - Lifts to 0.4 for the "Coming next" turn and for the outro, then fades out under the end card.
- **Music cue guidance:** from the Vol. 1 preset. The logo reveal (scene 2 start) is beat-locked to 10.02 s, on the beat grid that runs every 0.50 s from 3.02 s. The other reveals follow the narration words.
- **Audio-reactive treatment:** none. A narrated tour reads better with still UI.
- **SFX posture:** sparse and motion-matched:
  - soft impacts on scene changes, rotating three takes;
  - clicks on the POS taps;
  - a card slide on roadmap cards;
  - a bell on the two logo moments;
  - a soft bong on approvals and "Sale Recorded".
- **Restraint rule:** never over the voice at more than about 0.3. No synthesised tones.

## Voiceover script

Voice: Kokoro `af_heart`. One file per scene, in `composition/assets/vo/`.

1. Every day, Nigerian businesses sell, restock and pay their staff. And most of it lives in a notebook, a bank alert, and a WhatsApp chat.
2. Meet CACU. One place to sell, track your stock, run your team, and keep real books, without needing to know accounting.
3. Setting up takes minutes. Name your business, tell CACU whether you sell products, services, or both, and it shapes itself around how you work.
4. Add your products and services. Stock only changes through recorded movements, and big adjustments wait for an owner's approval. So nothing quietly disappears.
5. At the till, prices come from your catalogue, not from whoever is holding the phone. Ring up a cash or transfer sale, and it's recorded the moment you confirm.
6. Selling on credit? Issue an invoice, record part payments as they come in, and always know exactly who still owes you.
7. Supplier bills, payments and everyday expenses work the same way. Made a mistake? An owner or admin reverses it, with a reason. Nothing is ever deleted.
8. When you're ready, Start your books asks plain questions. How much cash is in the till? Who owes you? Who do you owe? You approve the answers, and your books begin.
9. From then on, every sale, receipt and bill posts balanced double-entry, automatically, on a standard Nigerian chart of accounts reviewed by a chartered accountant.
10. Trial balance. General ledger. Profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow, with comparatives, ready as PDF or Excel. And your stock is valued at weighted-average cost.
11. Invite your team as admins, editors or viewers. Keep staff records, track attendance, and prepare payroll on the 2026 Nigeria Tax Act bands, with every run approved by an owner or admin.
12. Behind it all, every business's data stays separate, posted records can't be quietly changed, and uploads are checked before anyone sees them.
13. And this is just the beginning. Here's what's coming next.
14. Transfers that wait until you confirm the money really arrived. An installable app that keeps selling when the network drops. And three plans, Founder, Innovator and Empire, paid through Paystack.
15. Purchase orders and stock transfers. Bookings with deposits and WhatsApp reminders. Cashier shifts, roles for cashiers and accountants, bank reconciliation, budgets, and VAT.
16. Ask CACU how much profit you made this month, and get the answer from your real books. See how ready your business is for funding, and share it with a lender only when you choose. Then payroll posting, e-invoicing, and new countries.
17. CACU. Sell. CACU keeps the books.
