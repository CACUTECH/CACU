# Hyperframes Composition Brief: CACU

## Objective

A narrated launch-tour film for CACU that covers every live feature and every planned feature. The user asked for a long film of about 3 minutes, which overrides brag's usual 15–25 s limit.

## Output

- **Composition:** `composition/` (index.html plus 17 scenes in `compositions/s01.html` to `s17.html`), generated by `work/build.py`.
- **Render:** `brag.mp4`, landscape 1920×1080 at 30 fps, 193.3 s.

## Creative direction

- **Tone:** polished. A calm product tour for owners, part launch film, part demo.
- **Hook:** "You sell all day. Who keeps the books?", followed by a notebook page, a bank alert and a WhatsApp message.
- **Outro:** the logo, "Books an owner can trust." and "Sell. CACU keeps the books."
- **Avoid:**
  - generic SaaS language;
  - invented capabilities;
  - roadmap dates (they are planning estimates only).

## Audio

- **Voice:** Kokoro `af_heart`, generated with `npx hyperframes tts`, one file per scene in `assets/vo/`. The voice is the clock: each slot is lead-in + line + tail, and reveals are anchored to the words that name them.
- **Music:**
  - Part 1, live today (0 to 136.5 s): "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 1" (Sascha Ende, CC BY 4.0), entering at 14.0 s into the track. The scene 2 logo is beat-locked to about 10.03 s.
  - Part 2, coming next (135.87 s to the end): "Vol. 12", entering at its 8.19 s beat.
  - Volume lanes: 0.45 on the opening title, 0.14 under the voice, 0.40 for the logo moment, the "Coming next" turn and the outro, then a 3 s fade out.
  - The credit appears on the end card.
- **SFX** (bundled Kenney CC0 files):
  - soft impacts on scene changes, rotating three takes;
  - clicks on taps and button presses;
  - a card slide on card reveals;
  - a bong on approvals and confirmations;
  - a bell on the two logo moments.

  Each lands one frame after its picture.
- **Audio-reactive treatment:** none, on purpose. A narrated tour reads better with still UI.
