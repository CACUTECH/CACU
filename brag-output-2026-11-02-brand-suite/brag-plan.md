## Claim boundaries
All modules are covered in features.md. Appointments, jobs, storefront, offline sync, subscription billing, credit, integrations, community and advanced analytics currently show unavailable pages and are not sold as working features. Payroll drafts are not accounting postings or payouts. AI and accounting require setup. Financial close controls are supported pilot workflows, not full IFRS certification.
